## Jank Minecraft Mods

A small collection of Minecraft mods I've made for me and my friends.

Mostly some tweaks and things that we couldn't find mods to fix, so I decided to make them myself.

All mods target Minecraft **1.20.1** on **Forge**.

### Mods

- **[Amphithere Fix](amphithere-ducktales-fix/)** - Quality-of-life fixes for Ice and Fire Amphitheres. After a midair dismount they no longer fly up into the sky and become unreachable, instead gliding down to land nearby; they no longer get stuck hovering frozen midair when dismounted while commanded to sit; and the Amphithere's body no longer twitches sideways in flight. Requires Ice and Fire.

- **[Dismount Rebind](dismount-rebind/)** - Adds a separate, rebindable Dismount key, decoupling dismounting from the Sneak key. Client-side only.

- **[Spawn Control](spawncontrol/)** - Controls natural mob spawning via configurable spawn-weight overrides and chance-based spawn rejection.
